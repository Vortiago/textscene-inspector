# WI-72: Subsurface Scattering Enable Flag (StandardMaterial3D)

**Status**: Deferred
**Priority**: Low
**Complexity**: High
**Impact**: Light penetration for skin, wax, organic materials

## Overview

Subsurface scattering simulates light penetrating and scattering inside translucent materials. Godot's `FEATURE_SUBSURFACE_SCATTERING` → `subsurf_scatter_enabled`.

**TSCN Properties**:
- `subsurf_scatter_enabled` - Enable flag
- `subsurf_scatter_strength` - Effect intensity
- `subsurf_scatter_texture` - Per-pixel control
- `subsurf_scatter_skin_mode` - Optimize for human skin

**Three.js Support**: Not in MeshStandardMaterial. Requires custom shader with light scattering simulation.

## Defer Reason
