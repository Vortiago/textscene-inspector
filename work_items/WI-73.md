# WI-73: Subsurface Transmittance Enable Flag (StandardMaterial3D)

**Status**: Deferred
**Priority**: Low
**Complexity**: High
**Impact**: Enhanced subsurface scattering with transmittance

## Overview

Subsurface transmittance enhances subsurface scattering with light transmission through thin materials. Godot's `FEATURE_SUBSURFACE_TRANSMITTANCE` → `subsurf_scatter_transmittance_enabled`.

**TSCN Properties**:
- `subsurf_scatter_transmittance_enabled` - Enable flag
- `subsurf_scatter_transmittance_depth` - Depth of effect
- `subsurf_scatter_transmittance_color` - Tint color
- `subsurf_scatter_transmittance_boost` - Intensity multiplier
- `subsurf_scatter_transmittance_texture` - Per-pixel control

**Three.js Support**: Not in MeshStandardMaterial. Requires advanced custom shader.
