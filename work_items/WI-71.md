# WI-71: Detail Map Enable Flag (StandardMaterial3D)

**Status**: Deferred
**Priority**: Low
**Complexity**: Moderate
**Impact**: Texture layering for surface detail overlay

## Overview

Detail maps blend a secondary texture over the base material for fine surface detail. Godot's `FEATURE_DETAIL` → `detail_enabled`.

**TSCN Properties**:
- `detail_enabled` - Enable flag
- `detail_albedo` - Detail color texture
- `detail_normal` - Detail normal map
- `detail_mask` - Blending mask
- `detail_blend_mode` - How to blend (mix, add, sub, mul)
- `detail_uv_layer` - UV or UV2

**Three.js Support**: Requires custom shader for dual texture blending.
