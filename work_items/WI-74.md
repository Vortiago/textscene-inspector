# WI-74: Bent Normal Mapping Enable Flag (StandardMaterial3D)

**Status**: Deferred
**Priority**: Low
**Complexity**: High
**Impact**: Advanced indirect lighting accuracy

## Overview

Bent normal mapping improves ambient occlusion and indirect lighting by specifying the average direction of incoming ambient light. Godot's `FEATURE_BENT_NORMAL_MAPPING` → `bent_normal_enabled`.

**TSCN Properties**:
- `bent_normal_enabled` - Enable flag
- `bent_normal_texture` - Bent normal map

**Three.js Support**: Not in MeshStandardMaterial. Requires custom shader with advanced lighting calculations.

## Defer Reason

- Very advanced lighting technique
- Requires custom shader modifications

- Rare usage (baked lighting workflows)
