# WI-69: Refraction Enable Flag (StandardMaterial3D)

**Status**: Deferred
**Priority**: Low
**Complexity**: High
**Impact**: Glass/water materials with light distortion

## Overview

Refraction distorts transparency based on light from behind objects, simulating glass, water, or other transparent materials. Godot's `FEATURE_REFRACTION` → `refraction_enabled`.

**TSCN Properties**:
- `refraction_enabled` - Enable flag
- `refraction_scale` - Distortion strength (float)
- `refraction_texture` - Per-pixel refraction control
- `refraction_texture_channel` - Channel selection

**Three.js Support**: Not in MeshStandardMaterial. Requires custom shader with screen-space refraction.
