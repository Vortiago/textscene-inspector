# WI-68: Anisotropy Enable Flag (StandardMaterial3D)

**Status**: Deferred
**Priority**: Low
**Complexity**: Moderate
**Impact**: Directional reflection (brushed metal, hair)

## Overview

Anisotropy changes the shape of specular reflections, creating directional highlights like brushed metal or hair. Godot's `FEATURE_ANISOTROPY` → `anisotropy_enabled`.

**TSCN Properties**:
- `anisotropy_enabled` - Enable flag
- `anisotropy` - Strength (float)
- `anisotropy_flowmap` - Texture controlling direction

**Three.js Support**: Requires `KHR_materials_anisotropy` extension or custom shader.
