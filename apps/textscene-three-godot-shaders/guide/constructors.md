# Constructors and conversions

The constructors of vectors and matrices, and the conversions between the scalar types.

Godot 4.6.3-stable (official), probed on llvmpipe (LLVM 20.1.2). Each table shows at most 8 of a probe's rows, picked to show as many mixes of zero, subnormal, normal, infinite and NaN values as fit. The real-GL test (`pnpm test:shaders:gpu`) checks every row, bit for bit, NaN lanes compared as NaN. Generated by `pnpm godot:shader-language`: do not edit by hand.


## Vector constructors

`vec3(a, b)` fills the lanes of the new vector with the lanes of its arguments, in order. One scalar argument fills every lane. The argument lanes must add up to the vector size, and have the vector's scalar type. A literal of another scalar type converts where Godot converts it: `vec2(1, 2)` is `vec2(1.0, 2.0)`.

The transpiler emits `vec3(a, b)`. ## Matrix constructors

`mat3(a, b, c)` takes one vector per column. `mat3(f)` puts the float `f` on the diagonal and zero elsewhere. three 0.187 converts one float to a matrix as an assignment GLSL refuses, so the transpiler writes the diagonal column by column.

The transpiler emits `mat3(a, b, c)`. Rows: `mat2(vec2,vec2)`, `mat3(vec3,vec3,vec3)`, `mat4(vec4,vec4,vec4,vec4)`, `mat2(float)`, `mat3(float)`, `mat4(float)`. Godot source: `servers/rendering/shader_language.cpp:2482`.

Lowered rows:

- `mat2(float)`: WebGL 2 lowered: diagonal of columns, WebGPURenderer lowered: diagonal of columns.
- `mat3(float)`: WebGL 2 lowered: diagonal of columns, WebGPURenderer lowered: diagonal of columns.
- `mat4(float)`: WebGL 2 lowered: diagonal of columns, WebGPURenderer lowered: diagonal of columns.

The other rows are native on WebGL 2 and on WebGPURenderer.
