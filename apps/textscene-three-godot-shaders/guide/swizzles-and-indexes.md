# Swizzles and indexes

Reading and writing lanes of vectors and columns of matrices.

Godot 4.6.3-stable (official), probed on llvmpipe (LLVM 20.1.2). Each table shows at most 8 of a probe's rows, picked to show as many mixes of zero, subnormal, normal, infinite and NaN values as fit. The real-GL test (`pnpm test:shaders:gpu`) checks every row, bit for bit, NaN lanes compared as NaN. Generated by `pnpm godot:shader-language`: do not edit by hand.


## Swizzles

`a.zyx` reads the named lanes of a vector of any scalar type, in any order, repeated or not, up to four. The letters come from one set: `xyzw`, `rgba` or `stpq`. Godot refuses a mix such as `a.xg`, and a letter past the vector, such as `.z` on a `vec2`. A swizzle with no letter twice can be assigned to: `a.zx = b` writes two lanes.

The transpiler emits `a.zyx`. Rows: `bvec2.xy`, `bvec2.rg`, `bvec2.st`, `ivec2.xy`, `ivec2.rg`, `ivec2.st`, `uvec2.xy`, `uvec2.rg`, `uvec2.st`, `vec2.xy`, `vec2.rg`, `vec2.st`, `bvec3.xyz`, `bvec3.rgb`, `bvec3.stp`, `ivec3.xyz`, `ivec3.rgb`, `ivec3.stp`, `uvec3.xyz`, `uvec3.rgb`, `uvec3.stp`, `vec3.xyz`, `vec3.rgb`, `vec3.stp`, `bvec4.xyzw`, `bvec4.rgba`, `bvec4.stpq`, `ivec4.xyzw`, `ivec4.rgba`, `ivec4.stpq`, `uvec4.xyzw`, `uvec4.rgba`, `uvec4.stpq`, `vec4.xyzw`, `vec4.rgba`, `vec4.stpq`. Godot source: `servers/rendering/shader_language.cpp:6877`.

Every row is native on WebGL 2 and on WebGPURenderer.

## Indexes `a[i]`

`a[i]` reads lane `i` of a vector or column `i` of a matrix, where `i` is an `int` or a `uint`. Godot refuses a constant index past the end. An index the shader computes past the end has no result GLSL defines. `a[i] = b` writes the lane or column.

The transpiler emits `a.element(i)`. Rows: `OP_INDEX`. Godot source: `servers/rendering/shader_language.cpp:1558`.

Every row is native on WebGL 2 and on WebGPURenderer.
