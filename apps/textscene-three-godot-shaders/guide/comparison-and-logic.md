# Comparison and logic

The comparisons, the logical operators and the conditional operator. Each gives one `bool`.

Godot 4.6.3-stable (official), probed on llvmpipe (LLVM 20.1.2, 256 bits). Each table shows at most 8 of a probe's rows, picked to show as many mixes of zero, subnormal, normal, infinite and NaN values as fit. The real-GL test (`pnpm test:shaders:gpu`) checks every row, bit for bit. Generated by `pnpm godot:shader-language`: do not edit by hand.


## `<`, `<=`, `>` and `>=`

Each compares two scalars of one type, `int`, `uint` or `float`. A comparison with NaN is false. Vectors compare with the functions `lessThan()` and the rest, not with these operators.

The transpiler emits `a.lessThan(b)`. Rows: `OP_LESS(int,int)`, `OP_LESS(uint,uint)`, `OP_LESS(float,float)`, `OP_LESS_EQUAL(int,int)`, `OP_LESS_EQUAL(uint,uint)`, `OP_LESS_EQUAL(float,float)`, `OP_GREATER(int,int)`, `OP_GREATER(uint,uint)`, `OP_GREATER(float,float)`, `OP_GREATER_EQUAL(int,int)`, `OP_GREATER_EQUAL(uint,uint)`, `OP_GREATER_EQUAL(float,float)`. Godot source: `servers/rendering/shader_language.cpp:1576`.

Every row is native on WebGL 2 and on WebGPURenderer.

## `&&`, `||` and `!`

`a && b`, `a || b` and `!a` on `bool` scalars. GLSL reads `b` only when `a` does not decide the result, so a step or an assignment inside `b` happens only then. The transpiler picks each argument's value after `b` with a select.

The transpiler emits `select(a, b, false)`. Rows: `OP_AND(bool,bool)`, `OP_OR(bool,bool)`, `OP_NOT(bool)`. Godot source: `servers/rendering/shader_language.cpp:1591`.

Lowered rows:

- `OP_AND(bool,bool)`: WebGL 2 lowered: select, WebGPURenderer lowered: select.
- `OP_OR(bool,bool)`: WebGL 2 lowered: select, WebGPURenderer lowered: select.

The other rows are native on WebGL 2 and on WebGPURenderer.

## The conditional operator `c ? a : b`

`c ? a : b` gives `a` when the `bool` `c` holds, and `b` otherwise. `a` and `b` have one type, any value type. GLSL runs one side, so a step or an assignment on the other side does not happen. Godot keeps a negated constant in a part as it reads it and negates the constant in place (`shader_language.cpp:7832`), so `c ? -vec2(1.0) : x` draws `vec2(1.0)` when `c` holds. The transpiler draws it as Godot does.

The transpiler emits `select(c, a, b)`. Rows: `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,bool,bool)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,bvec2,bvec2)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,bvec3,bvec3)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,bvec4,bvec4)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,int,int)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,ivec2,ivec2)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,ivec3,ivec3)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,ivec4,ivec4)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,uint,uint)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,uvec2,uvec2)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,uvec3,uvec3)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,uvec4,uvec4)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,float,float)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,vec2,vec2)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,vec3,vec3)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,vec4,vec4)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,mat2,mat2)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,mat3,mat3)`, `OP_SELECT_IF(bool,mat4,mat4)`, `OP_SELECT_ELSE`.
